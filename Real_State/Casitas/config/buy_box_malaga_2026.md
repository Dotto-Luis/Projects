# Buy Box: Málaga Real Estate Investment Criteria 2026

## Executive Summary
Investment thesis focused on value-add residential properties in Málaga (Spain) for owner-occupancy and rental income potential. Target portfolio: mid-market residential units (1-3 bedrooms) with strong location fundamentals and renovation upside.

---

## 1. MARKET OVERVIEW

**Geography:** Málaga City + immediate suburbs (Torremolinos, Benalmádena, Mijas)  
**Currency:** EUR  
**Target Investor Profile:** Individual buyer or small fund  
**Investment Horizon:** 5-10 years  
**Exit Strategy:** Owner-occupancy or rental at €8-12/m²/month

---

## 2. GEOGRAPHIC ZONES & LOCATION SCORING

### PREMIUM ZONES (location_score: 85-100)
High walkability, proximity to amenities, strong rental demand, coastal/city center

- **Centro/Historic Center** — Walkable, tourism, bars/restaurants
- **El Palo/Pedregalejo** — Beach access, young professionals, high rental yield
- **Málaga Puerto/Waterfront** — Renovated, amenities, premium pricing
- **Calvario** — Established, schools, local commerce
- **Limonar** — Upscale residential, families, services nearby

**Score boost:** +10 if within 500m of beach, +5 if metro/bus nearby

### ACCEPTABLE ZONES (location_score: 70-84)
Developing neighborhoods, still investment-grade, good rental potential

- **Lagunilla**
- **Parque Ayala**
- **El Torcal**
- **Capuchinos**
- **Teatinos**
- **Torremolinos (center)**
- **Benalmádena (center)**

### CAUTION ZONES (location_score: 50-69)
Avoid unless exceptional price or property condition

- **Outer suburbs** (>10km from city center)
- **Industrial zones**
- **Areas with high unemployment** (>15%)

### DISQUALIFY ZONES (location_score: <50)
Do not invest

- **Known drug-trafficking areas**
- **Extreme periphery** (>15km)
- **Contaminated/pollution zones**

## 3. PROPERTY TYPE PREFERENCES

### PREFERRED (type_score: 80-100)
- **Piso (Apartment)** — Liquid market, manageable maintenance
  - 1-2 bedrooms: 40-70 m²
  - 2-3 bedrooms: 70-120 m²
- **Ático (Penthouse/Top-floor)** — Prestige, no upstairs neighbors
- **Townhouse (Casa Adosada)** — Garden, privacy, still manageable

### ACCEPTABLE (type_score: 60-79)
- **Chalet** (single-family house) — Only if <200m², recent build, or fully renovated
- **Studio/Estudio** — Only if <30 m², in premium zone, rental-focused

### AVOID (type_score: <60)
- **Local/Commercial** — Not residential
- **Land/Terreno** — Requires construction
- **Finca/Rural property** — Too far, low rental yield
- **Obra Nueva (off-plan)** — Execution risk

## 5. PHYSICAL CHARACTERISTICS

### Bedrooms & Bathrooms

| Configuration | Min | Acceptable | Ideal | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 bed | 1 bath | 1-1.5 bath | 1.5 bath | 2 bath |
| 2 bed | 1 bath | 1-2 bath | 2 bath | 2.5 bath |
| 3 bed | 1.5 bath | 2-2.5 bath | 2.5 bath | 3 bath |

**Scoring:**
- +15: Balanced bed/bath ratio (1 bed = 1+ bath, 2 bed = 2 bath, 3 bed = 2.5+ bath)
- +0: Acceptable but not ideal
- -10: Bath deficit (e.g., 3 bed, 1 bath)

### Square Footage (Constructed m²)

**Minimum density:** 35 m²/person (i.e., 1-bed min 35 m², 2-bed min 60 m²)  
**Maximum:** 150 m² (prefer <120 m² for rental velocity)

| Type | Min | Acceptable | Ideal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Studio | 25 m² | 30-35 m² | 35 m² |
| 1-bed | 40 m² | 50-65 m² | 55-65 m² |
| 2-bed | 65 m² | 75-100 m² | 80-90 m² |
| 3-bed | 95 m² | 110-140 m² | 100-120 m² |

**Kill criterion:** <30 m² (studio) or >150 m² single-unit (unless chalet/villa).

### Floor & Access

**Preferred:**
- Ground floor + patio/garden (+10)
- Mid-floor (2-4) with exterior walls (+5)
- Top floor (ático) with views (+15)

**Avoid:**
- Basement/semi-basement (-15, flooding risk)
- 5th+ floor without elevator (-20, aging cohort, emergency egress)

**Elevator:** +10 if present on 3rd+ floor. Not critical for 1-2 story buildings.

### Orientation & Natural Light

**Optimal:** South or Southeast facing (winter sun, passive heat)  
**Acceptable:** East or West  
**Avoid:** North-only (-15, dark, cold)

**Dual-exposure (esquina):** +5  
**Terrace/Balcony:** +10 (if >8 m²)  
**Patio:** +5 (if accessible)

**Bonus points:**

- +3: Insulation upgraded

**Penalties:**
- -10: Asbestos present

## 7. INVESTMENT PARAMETERS

### Rental Yield Target (if buy-to-rent)

**Gross yield formula:** (Annual Rent / Purchase Price) × 100

- **Acceptable:** 4-6% gross yield (€2,500/month for €500K property)
- **Strong:** 6-8% gross yield
- **Exceptional:** 8%+ gross yield

**Málaga benchmarks (2026):**
- Premium zones: 4-5% (€12-15/m²/month)
- Acceptable zones: 5-7% (€10-14/m²/month)
- Caution zones: 7-10% (€8-12/m²/month)

**Score calculation:**
- Estimated yield = (estimated_monthly_rent × 12) / purchase_price
- +20: >7% yield
- +10: 5-7% yield
- +0: 3-5% yield
- -10: <3% yield (speculative/appreciation-only)

### Comparable Market Analysis (Comps)

- Compare against 3-5 recently sold properties (same zone, type, ±20% size)
- If listing price >10% above comps → price_penalty = -15
- If listing price 10% below comps → bonus_oportunidad = +15

## 8. KILL CRITERIA (Automatic Disqualification)

If ANY of these are true, recommend = "DISCARD" immediately, score = 0.

1. **Price >€5,000/m² (non-penthouse)** — Overpriced
2. **Price <€800/m² (any zone)** — Distressed/hidden defects likely
3. **Located in known problematic zone** (drug trafficking, extreme crime)
4. **Structural damage** (cracks, subsidence, foundation issues)
5. **Flooded area** (basement, flood-prone zone, no insurance)
6. **Rental status unclear** — "Alquilado" or "Con inquilinos" but seller can't guarantee possession
7. **Disputed ownership** — Multiple owners, lien, pending litigation
8. **Illegal construction** (informal extension, no permits)
9. **Contaminated land** — Industrial heritage, pollution records
10. **Location in condemned building** (próxima demolición)
11. **No water supply or utilities unavailable**
12. **HOA fees >€300/month** (unless luxury complex)
13. **Listed as "nuda propiedad" (bare ownership)** or usufruct complications
14. **Age >50y + major systems untouched** (cost of complete overhaul exceeds value)

## 9. SCORING FRAMEWORK

### Component Scores (0-100 each)

**1. Location Score (25% weight)**
- Zone classification (premium/acceptable/caution): 50-100
- Walkability & proximity to services: 0-20
- Transit access: 0-10
- Beach/park proximity: 0-10

**2. Property Condition Score (25% weight)**
- Construction year: 0-95
- Current condition: 0-90
- Systems & efficiency: 0-15
- Environmental issues: -20 to 0

**3. Distribution & Functionality Score (20% weight)**
- Bedrooms/bathrooms balance: 0-20
- Square footage adequacy: 0-20
- Floor/access quality: 0-20
- Natural light & orientation: 0-10
- Outdoor space (terrace/garden): 0-10

**4. Financial/Market Score (20% weight)**
- Price-to-market alignment: 0-25
- Rental yield potential: 0-20
- HOA/tax reasonableness: 0-15
- Appreciation potential: 0-10

**5. Intangible Score (10% weight, bonus/penalty)**
- Unique features (charm, views, rarity): +10 to -5
- Seller motivation (motivated = bonus): +5 to -5
- Bidding war risk: -10 to 0

### Final Score Calculation

```
score_total = (
  location_score × 0.25 +
  condition_score × 0.25 +
  distribution_score × 0.20 +
  financial_score × 0.20 +
  intangible_score × 0.10
) + bonuses - penalties

# Cap at 0-100
score_total = max(0, min(100, score_total))
```

### Score Interpretation & Recommendation

| Score | Recommendation | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **90-100** | **STRONG OPPORTUNITY** | Serious site visit, prepare offer |
| **80-89** | **WORTH VISITING** | Schedule viewing, negotiate |
| **70-79** | **CONDITIONAL** | Visit if price drops 5-10% or repairs negotiated |
| **60-69** | **MARGINAL** | Only if seeking specific niche (e.g., rental arbitrage) |
| **<60 or Kill** | **DISCARD** | Pass, move to next prospect |

### Recommendation Labels

- **oportunidad fuerte** = Score 85+, no kill criteria
- **vale visita** = Score 70-84, worth in-person inspection
- **solo si precio excelente** = Score 60-69, negotiation-dependent
- **descartar** = Score <60 OR kill criteria triggered

## 11. MÁLAGA MARKET CONTEXT 2026

### Macro Factors
- Tourism dependency: High seasonal volatility, but structural growth
- Rental yield: 4-7% gross (competitive vs. Madrid/Barcelona)
- Population: Growing tech/digital nomad influx (younger, higher rents)
- Infrastructure: New metro, improved connectivity
- Climate: Year-round rental appeal (tourism + expats)

### Micro Zones Hot Spots
- **Málaga Centro (walking distance to cathedral):** Premium, but high prices
- **El Palo:** Young professionals, restaurants, good rental velocity
- **Benalmádena Centro:** Tourist hub, steady rental yield
- **Limonar:** Upscale families, schools, lower speculative risk
- **Parque Ayala:** Emerging, good value, less saturated

### Yield Reality Check
- **Budget properties:** Often rented to tourists (Airbnb-style) → higher gross yield (8-10%) but higher vacancy & management
- **Mid-market:** Owner-occupancy + vacation rentals → 5-7% yield, more stable
- **Premium:** Long-term professional rentals → 3-5% yield, lower turnover
